I grew up in a settlement most maps don&rsquo;t name. By the time I was old enough to ask
where the visitors went after they finished taking photos, the answer was obvious: home, with
their photos. Some of them left checks. None of them left jobs.

I want to tell you the story of era92 the way I&rsquo;d tell it to a friend over coffee, in
five honest minutes.

## What I noticed

For twenty years, smart, generous people came through my neighborhood with the same playbook.
Build a school. Drill a well. Fund a clinic. Train a class. Most of them meant well. Some of
them were excellent. And almost none of the work outlasted the funding cycle.

There was a pattern I couldn&rsquo;t unsee: the moment the donors moved on, the project
calcified. People I knew &mdash; smart, talented people &mdash; ended up running the empty
shell of someone else&rsquo;s good intention. They wanted careers. We kept giving them
programs.

> Charities raise money to keep people alive. Companies raise money to make money. What if we
> needed something in between &mdash; something that uses commerce to do what charity
> couldn&rsquo;t?

## What we&rsquo;re building

era92 is a holding company. That sounds dry; it&rsquo;s the most important word in the
business.

The holding company owns four ventures, and each venture does one job:

- **Elevate** trains young people from settlements like the one I grew up in &mdash; six
  months, free, with the equipment and mentorship to make the training actually stick.
- **Creative** is the agency that hires the graduates. It serves international clients and
  generates the revenue that subsidizes Elevate from year two onward.
- **Fund** is licensed microfinance. It lends to graduates building their own ventures and to
  established adult entrepreneurs in our communities.
- **Hub** is the physical infrastructure &mdash; the buildings, the broadband, the rooms
  where it all happens.

That&rsquo;s the model in four sentences. The rest of the work is making it true.

## Why I&rsquo;m writing this

Because I want you to hold us to it. If you&rsquo;re here from a foundation, an investment
cohort, a parish, or a curiosity click &mdash; I&rsquo;d rather you start with the model, in
plain language, than with a polished mission video.

We&rsquo;ll write often. Some of it will be field notes from places like Bidi Bidi.
Some will be investor updates with numbers. Some will be longer letters like this one. All of
it is meant to be read by someone deciding whether to bet on us.

Thanks for being here.
