## Narrative

Q1 was a build quarter. We didn&rsquo;t launch anything new; we strengthened the things we
already shipped. Two themes stood out.

> The work we&rsquo;re proudest of this quarter is the work no one will see for a year:
> infrastructure, vetting, and the boring discipline of saying no to projects that
> don&rsquo;t fit.

First, the Bidi Bidi cohort proved that the model holds in a refugee-settlement context with
some retuning around bandwidth and placement. We&rsquo;ll publish the revised Hub-buildout
template in Q2.

Second, era92 Creative crossed a threshold we&rsquo;ve been working toward for two years:
its trailing twelve-month gross margin is now sufficient to cover the operating cost of
running one full Elevate cohort from revenue alone. The model is starting to prove itself
as a self-sustaining system, not a perpetually-subsidized program.
